The Investment: What It Actually Costs

Here's the number every serious investor wants first: starting a EuroKids preschool franchise requires an investment of ₹15–20 lakhs*. This figure isn't fixed — it varies depending on the size of your centre and the city you're operating in. A centre in a metro city with higher real estate costs will sit at the higher end of that range, while smaller cities and towns can come in closer to the ₹15 lakh mark.

This amount typically covers:

  • Infrastructure setup and interior design in line with EuroKids' ambiance guidelines
  • Furniture, teaching aids, and classroom equipment
  • Curriculum materials, welcome kits, and theme-based learning tools
  • Initial training and induction support for you and your teaching staff

You will also need a minimum space of 1,500 sq ft to set up a centre that meets EuroKids' infrastructure and safety standards. This isn't a bureaucratic requirement — adequate space is what allows the brand to maintain its recommended 1:6 adult-to-child ratio, which is one of the reasons parents trust the EuroKids name in the first place.

If you're evaluating this against other business options, the appeal is straightforward: this is a long-term vision business, not a quick-flip venture. Preschools build community trust and referral-based admissions over years, which means your investment compounds in value the longer you operate.

Common Questions First-Time Investors Ask

How much do I need to invest in a EuroKids preschool franchise?

Between ₹15–20 lakhs*, depending on your centre's size and city.

Do you help with setting up infrastructure and sourcing equipment?

Yes. Guidance is provided for ambience design, safety compliance, and sourcing furniture, books, toys, and classroom equipment.

I'm new to running a preschool — will I get operational training?

Yes. EuroKids provides structured induction training and ongoing operational support so first-time entrepreneurs aren't left managing day-to-day operations without guidance.

How do I know if my location is right for a preschool franchise?

This depends on local demographics, nearby residential density, competing schools, and accessibility — EuroKids' team typically evaluates this as part of the franchise discussion process.

How long does it take to set up a centre?

Timeline varies by city and how quickly the required space and approvals come together, but EuroKids' structured setup process is designed to minimize delays compared to setting up a preschool independently.

How many staff will I need to run my centre?

Staffing depends on your enrolled student count, based on EuroKids' recommended 1:6 adult-to-child ratio — for reference, roughly four teachers, two support staff, a centre head, and security personnel per 40-plus students is a common structure.

Is This the Right Business for You?

Before reaching out for franchise details, it helps to be honest about your own motivation. If you're looking for financial independence through a business you can grow over years, a first business that comes with a support system instead of a blank slate, or simply a way to diversify into a recession-resilient sector — a EuroKids preschool franchise fits squarely into that category.

What it isn't: a passive income stream you can run without involvement, or a get-rich-quick investment. Like any real business, your returns depend on how actively you manage admissions, staff quality, and parent relationships in your local market — EuroKids gives you the system and the brand; you still need to run the centre.
